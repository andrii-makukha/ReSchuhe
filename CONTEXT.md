# ReSchuhe Context

This glossary contains only canonical ReSchuhe-specific language. It intentionally excludes implementation details and unconfirmed business concepts.

## Language

**ReSchuhe**:
The canonical name of the brand and digital product represented by this repository. Its commercial offering, audience, and market positioning are not yet defined.
_Avoid_: Alternate capitalization; treating “showroom,” “store,” “marketplace,” or “service” as a confirmed product type before `docs/product/PRODUCT.md` defines it.

**Product Experience**:
The complete digital experience delivered under the ReSchuhe name, independent of a particular page, device, release, or future delivery channel.
_Avoid_: Website when the broader experience is meant; platform for an individual screen or feature.

**Public Release Slice**:
The smallest production-ready portion of the Product Experience that combines an approved visual baseline with one complete, useful journey for real people and remains viable while later capabilities are added.
_Avoid_: Demo, prototype, placeholder landing page, full Product Experience, or an unscoped MVP.

**Creative Studio**:
The separate workspace where ReSchuhe creative source work is produced and curated. It is not an application package, public asset directory, or second software repository.
_Avoid_: Media library, app assets, public folder.

**Source Asset**:
An editable or high-fidelity creative input kept in the Creative Studio, including working files and variants. A Source Asset is not automatically safe or suitable for product use.
_Avoid_: Final asset, production asset.

**Approved Asset**:
A creative output that has passed brand, quality, rights, provenance, and intended-use review and may be optimized for the Product Experience.
_Avoid_: Draft, generated output, or selected variant unless approval is recorded.

**Design Baseline**:
The documented combination of approved design tokens, component states, interaction rules, and accessibility expectations used to implement the Product Experience.
_Avoid_: Raw Stitch screen, moodboard, isolated mockup.
