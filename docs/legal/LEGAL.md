# ReSchuhe Legal Readiness

Status: Decision checklist only | Last reviewed: 2026-08-07

This document is not legal advice and does not declare compliance. Qualified review must be based on the confirmed business entity, markets, users, features, data, and commercial model.

## Information required before legal review

- Operating legal entity and responsible contact.
- Countries where the product is operated, marketed, and available.
- Audience characteristics, including whether minors or other protected groups are in scope.
- Product offering, commercial relationship, pricing, fulfillment, cancellation, and support model.
- Every category of personal or device data collected, purpose, recipient, location, retention, and deletion path.
- Cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising, personalization, and third-party embeds.
- User-generated content, accounts, communications, payments, bookings, or other regulated flows.
- Ownership and licensing of copy, code, fonts, images, video, trademarks, models, and generated media.

## Readiness areas

Depending on confirmed scope and jurisdiction, obtain review for:

- operator identity and required business disclosures;
- privacy notice and data-subject/user request process;
- consent and preference management;
- terms, purchase/service conditions, cancellation, returns, warranties, and dispute handling;
- accessibility obligations;
- intellectual-property, trademark, likeness, model-release, and asset-license rights;
- marketing claims, testimonials, promotions, email, and advertising;
- processor/service-provider agreements and cross-border data handling;
- retention, deletion, incident response, and security responsibilities.

This list is a discovery aid, not a statement that every item applies.

## Repository boundary

- Keep approved public-facing policy text here only when it is intended to be versioned and published with the product.
- Do not store confidential advice, signed agreements, identity documents, private addresses, signatures, or personal case data in this public repository.
- Store evidence and restricted legal records in an access-controlled system selected by the project owner.

## Launch gate

Production launch requires the project owner to confirm that applicable legal review is complete, public notices match actual product behavior, consent choices work as described, data/service inventories are current, required owners are assigned, and all production assets have documented usage rights.

An early Public Release Slice is still a production launch. Review applies to the countries, users,
claims, data, assets, and behavior actually included in that slice; unimplemented future scope does
not reduce obligations for what is public.

## Open decisions

All applicability remains open until the product definition, entity, markets, audience, data behavior, and launch scope are confirmed.
